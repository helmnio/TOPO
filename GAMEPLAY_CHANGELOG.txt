============================================================
MAJOR GAMEPLAY VERSION CHANGE — ICONS + ROTATION (V2)
============================================================

Status: Active Daily ruleset during development
Previous ruleset: Colour Only (V1)
New ruleset: Icons + Rotation (V2)

WHAT CHANGED

- The Icons + Rotation ruleset now powers the Daily TOPO.
- Six map symbols are generated from palette groups used by the puzzle.
- Symbols appear in fragments and must also be placed on the completed map.
- Fragments begin at quarter-turn rotations and can be rotated clockwise.
- One fragment carries a north/navigation marker and begins north-up.
- Fragments are placed on the map as whole pieces: tap one to load it, drag it,
  rotate it, then tap elsewhere to drop it. Tap a placed piece to pick it up
  again. Square-by-square colouring and group rotation were replaced by this.
- Rotate with the button, a two-finger twist, a double-tap or the R key. Remove
  with the button, by dragging off the map, or with Delete.
- Squares where placed fragments disagree on a colour get a red outline.
- Symbols stay on the fragments; tap a symbol, then a map square, to place one.
- Each puzzle has three overlap hints.
- Correct completion requires the original north-up map orientation, correct
  colours, and correct symbol positions.
- Daily, current-streak, and lifetime hint usage is recorded in Stats.

PRESERVED LEGACY GAMEPLAY

The original Colour Only ruleset has not been deleted. Both rule profiles live
in src/config/gameplayRules.js:

- color-only-v1: original colour placement gameplay
- icon-rotate-v2: symbols, rotations, north marker, and hints

ACTIVE_DAILY_RULESET controls which profile is used for the Daily game. This
keeps the original gameplay available for comparison, regression testing, or a
future classic mode without maintaining a separate public game mode today.

STATS MIGRATION

Existing Daily totals, wins, streaks, accuracy, and timing data are preserved.
Hint fields are additive and default to zero for games completed before V2.
